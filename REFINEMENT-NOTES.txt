SOUND
58 new sound effects and variants use layered impacts, scrapes and rustling.

A fixed voice pool and peak limiter prevent runaway overlap.
Music/Effects/Ambience volume controls remain independent.

MOVEMENT AND LIGHT
Measured step height replaces the fixed 42 cm upward shove.
Camera compensation eases ledge ascent; ordinary hills use normal floor motion.
Reduced head bob and constant uphill walking speed.
Flashlight reaches 38 m, casts shadows, has a softer edge and warmer tint.
Low battery dims smoothly without flashing. F toggles; J uses battery packs.

PATROLS
Scheduled inspections visit existing shelters/camps and scan three nearby points.
Occupancy accumulates in 24 m areas; brief visits are ignored.
After roughly four minutes, an area becomes eligible for an activity inspection.
Heat slowly decays after departure. An inspected hot area gets a five-minute cooldown.
Dogs search the area, not a continuously tracked player position.
Inspections use the normal sight/hearing rules and can turn into pursuit.
The inspection dispatcher won't add patrols when three machines are already active.
Occupied-area pressure continues even around a claimed shelter.

POWERED WORKBENCH
At a workbench, scroll to Upgrade this workbench.
Cost: 12 scrap + 3 circuits, paid once per station.
The upgraded station needs a running claimed generator within 30 m.
The crafting screen shows ONLINE or OFFLINE.
Grenades, mines and battery packs require the upgraded powered station.
A small station indicator changes color with upgrade/power state.
Existing crafted workbenches can be upgraded too.
Upgrades persist through saves, including after dismantling other buildings.
